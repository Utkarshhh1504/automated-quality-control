About Us

Automated quality control (AQC) involves using technology and systems to monitor and maintain product quality throughout the manufacturing process. Here are some key points;

•  Ensure that the raw materials meet the required quality standards before entering the production process. Automated systems such as spectrometers, X-ray fluorescence (XRF), and Near-Infrared (NIR) sensors can identify material.
•  Sensors and Cameras: Used to detect deviations in size, shape, color, and other physical attributes. Provide high-speed inspection and can be integrated with robotic systems for real-time adjustments.
•  Process Control Software: Analyzes data from various sensors to ensure parameters stay within acceptable ranges. Ensure the finished products meet quality standards before they are shipped to customers.
